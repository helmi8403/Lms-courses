# ITIL Version 5 Foundation: learning course

**Latest edition:** [detailed draft v0.2: manuscript, PDF and PowerPoint](editions/v0.2/README.md), 15 November 2026. The modules and original full exports below are retained as the earlier v0.1 edition.

The latest edition remains independently authored and has unresolved criterion-by-criterion and official-source coverage gaps. No practice exams, videos or labs.

Learn how digital products and services create value, and how to improve their management using a single fictional case: **Harbour Learning**, a training provider building a reliable online learning service.

## Scope and honesty

This course addresses the seven Foundation subject areas identified in the PeopleCert-authored February 2026 syllabus. It is a substantial first teaching draft, not a claim that every examination criterion has been verified. Read [coverage and remaining work](COVERAGE.md) before using it for certification preparation. It does not replace the official publication, licensed training, or advanced practice qualifications.

## Learning route

1. [Orientation, value and relationships](modules/01-value-and-relationships.md): orientation; vocabulary; service offerings; relationships.
2. [Four dimensions and lifecycle](modules/02-dimensions-and-lifecycle.md): balanced design; eight lifecycle activities.
3. [Principles and governance](modules/03-principles-and-governance.md): decision principles; oversight and accountability.
4. [Operations, practices and improvement](modules/04-operations-and-improvement.md): delivery/operations; capability; continual improvement.
5. [Value streams](modules/05-value-streams.md): end-to-end flow and measurement.
6. [AI and complementary frameworks](modules/06-ai-and-frameworks.md): responsible AI; DevOps and PRINCE2.
7. [Integrated course review](modules/07-capstone-and-assessment.md): a connected explanatory case and summary.

Suggested self-study schedule: seven sessions of 60–90 minutes, with a concluding integrated review. This is an author recommendation, not an official training-duration requirement. Each session: explain the concepts in your own words, apply them to the case, read the worked example and its reasoning.

## How to judge your understanding

You should be able to explain why a decision improves an outcome, identify who benefits or bears a risk, recognize the effect on all four dimensions, and propose evidence that would demonstrate improvement. Memorizing a label without reasoning is insufficient.
