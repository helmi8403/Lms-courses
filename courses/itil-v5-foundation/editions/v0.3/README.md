# ITIL Version 5 Foundation: illustrated edition v0.3

Detailed teaching manuscript baseline: v0.2. This edition adds seven original instructional visuals, while retaining all seven modules and expanded educational notes.

## Delivery files
- [Volume 1](ITIL-Version-5-Foundation-Illustrated-v0.3-Volume-1.pdf) (source pages 1–7), [Volume 2](ITIL-Version-5-Foundation-Illustrated-v0.3-Volume-2.pdf) (source pages 8–14), and [Volume 3](ITIL-Version-5-Foundation-Illustrated-v0.3-Volume-3.pdf) (source pages 15–35): 35 pages total, including full-size landscape teaching-diagram pages beside the relevant modules.
- [PowerPoint](ITIL-Version-5-Foundation-Illustrated-v0.3.pptx): 114 slides, with editable teaching text, native captions and notes. Diagram illustrations are embedded images.

## Visual coverage
1. Value co-creation: provider and consumer contributions and contextual outcomes.
2. Four dimensions: interacting perspectives on the same service, with native readable labels.
3. Nonlinear lifecycle: all eight activity labels, plus illustrative return paths.
4. Value system: principles and governance influence work, practices provide capabilities, outcome evidence supports continual improvement.
5. Restoration, investigation and improvement: separate responsibilities, with change-assessment boundary.
6. Value-stream time: fictional 30 minutes active, 90 waiting, 120 elapsed, 75% waiting. Ribbon widths are schematic, not proportional.
7. AI oversight: approved knowledge, draft, responsible review, expert escalation, authorized response and monitored feedback.

All seven are original conceptual teaching representations. They do not claim to reproduce official ITIL models, diagrams or stencil packs. Unverified official-model detail and 95-criterion completeness remain explicitly identified. No practice examination, video or lab was added.

## Provenance and checks
[Diagram manifest](diagrams.json) records repository-relative original PNG sources, SHA-256 hashes, captions, alt descriptions and teaching notes. Source PNGs remain unchanged. Delivery encoding reduces file size without changing conceptual content. Repeated PPTX image blobs are deduplicated losslessly.

All new diagrams were inspected for labels, intended relationships and readability. All slides rendered. PPTX package, geometry and font checks passed with no findings. No native PowerPoint application test was performed. The PDF is split at the Module 2/3 and Module 4/5 boundaries to fit the publishing transport. Extracted text and rendered pixels of every split page match the complete source PDF. Original manuscript page numbers remain unchanged; use PDF page positions for the volume ranges.
