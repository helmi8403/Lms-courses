## What the course actually includes

- A pilot explaining examination and credential-route details, with appointment-dependent facts explicitly qualified.
- Eleven further chapters, including an integrated fictional architecture narrative.
- Original explanations of constraints, tradeoffs and failure modes.
- A linked official-source register and editable chapter sources.
- No claim that the fictional examples were deployed, benchmarked or validated.

## Deliberately excluded by the owner

Practice exams, practice-exam questions, videos and labs. These are not unfinished promises. Explained worked examples remain instructional prose, not assessments.

## Remaining depth limitations

This first edition is approximately 5,000 words. It is not an exhaustive textbook. Detailed SKU limits, regional availability, tenant licensing, every authentication protocol, every service's HA implementation and detailed migration tools are not enumerated. They require targeted expansion and current documentation checks. Exact pricing, guarantees and product limits are deliberately not invented.

The publisher should label the PDF and PowerPoint as this edition, retain these limitations, and not describe the materials as an accredited or complete official course. A slide deck may summarize the manuscript but must not imply that its shorter format includes details it omits.
